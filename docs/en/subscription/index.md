# Use a Jego subscription on computers and phones

The Jego browser extension covers websites in Chrome and Edge. Members can also get a subscription URL and add Jego nodes to a compatible client, allowing other apps on a computer or phone to use a VPN connection.

## Get started in three steps

1. [Choose your device](/en/devices/) and find a currently supported client.
2. Open the client's [complete setup guide](/en/tool/) and install the app.
3. Copy your subscription from the Jego Control Panel, then follow the guide to import it, choose a node, and connect.

A subscription URL is simply a private link that lets the client download your node list.

## Subscription setup steps

| Task | Entry point |
|---|---|
| Choose a client for Windows, Mac, Android, or iPhone | [Choose by device](/en/devices/) |
| Install and configure a client you already chose | [Client tutorials](/en/tool/) |
| Copy, update, move, or repair a subscription | [Copy and update a subscription](/en/subscription/management) |
| Fix an import, empty node list, or connection | [Client troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/client) |

## Keep the subscription URL private

A subscription URL works like a private key: someone who has it may be able to download its node configuration. Keep it on your own devices and in trusted clients, and cover it completely in screenshots.

If it was sent to the wrong person or posted publicly, stop sharing it and ask Jego [Support](/en/guide/support) or use the Control Panel to replace it. Update every client after receiving the new URL.

::: warning Unsupported clients
Jego no longer supports v2rayN, v2rayNG, Loon, or OneClick. Their original guides remain available for existing users, but new users should choose a currently supported client from the device page.
:::
