# Jego privacy and data

::: info Official policy
This page explains [Jego's official Privacy Policy](https://jegocloud.com/policy) in simpler language. The policy is effective January 5, 2026. If this summary differs from the policy, follow the policy itself.
:::

## Information Jego processes

Jego needs a small amount of account and transaction information when you register, sign in, or purchase service:

| Information | Why it is needed | What the policy says |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Sign-in | Encrypted at rest on Akamai infrastructure in the United States |
| Password | Authentication | Hashed and stored on Akamai infrastructure in the United States |
| Subscription status | Service access | Encrypted at rest on Akamai infrastructure in the United States |
| Transaction ID | Payment verification | Encrypted at rest; payment-card or payment-account details stay with the payment processor |

The policy says data is sent over HTTPS/TLS, stored data is encrypted, and access is limited to authorized people. Jego does not sell personal data and does not use analytics, ads, pixels, or third-party tracking cookies.

It also says the Jego Service does not access browsing history, tabs, cookies, webpage content, network requests, or files, and does not record browsing activity, connection time, or location. These statements summarize Jego's policy; third-party clients and websites have their own privacy rules.

## Browser extension permissions

The official policy lists four permissions and purposes:

| Permission | Purpose stated by the policy |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Save settings locally |
| `proxy` | Route traffic through Jego servers |
| `alarms` | Refresh connection information on a schedule |
| `management` | Detect conflicting extensions |

These permissions save settings, change proxy routes, keep connection information updated, and identify other proxy extensions. See [Why Jego needs browser permissions](/en/guide/plugin-permissions-privacy) for details.

## Payments and deleting an account

- Payment-card or payment-account details are handled by payment processors. Jego receives the transaction ID used to confirm payment. The checkout page shows the processor and payment method for that order.
- The policy says data is stored in the United States and the Service is not intended for users under 13.
- Users can sign in to view their data and can request account deletion in Account Settings. For a security incident or inaccessible account, use [Support](/en/guide/support).

Installation, import, and update steps for the subscription service are in the matching [device guide](/en/subscription/#install-by-device).
