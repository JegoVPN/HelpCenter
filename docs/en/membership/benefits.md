# Membership System

## Paid Membership Benefits

1. When network blocking or service failures occur, the Jego team **prioritizes service for paid members**.
2. Paid members can use more, faster, and more stable nodes in the Jego extension.
3. Membership includes Jego subscription service for compatible computer and mobile clients. See the [computer and phone setup guide](/en/subscription/).

::: warning Free Service Note
Free routes have more users, so availability and load may vary. The Jego team continues to maintain the free service.
:::

::: info VIP Rules

### VIP Duration Is Cumulative

Purchased VIP duration is added directly to the account.

- Purchase 2 monthly cards → VIP duration = 31 + 31 = 62 days
- Purchase a monthly card with 3 VIP days remaining → VIP duration = 31 + 3 = 34 days

### VIP Level Is Cumulative

Each purchase increases the level by the corresponding amount:

| Monthly Card | Quarterly Card | Half-Year Card | Annual Card |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| +1 Level | +3 Levels | +6 Levels | +12 Levels |

**Examples**:

- New user buys a monthly card → Level = 0 + 1 = **1**
- Buys another monthly card → Level = 1 + 1 = **2**
- Buys an annual card → Level = 2 + 12 = **14**

### Purpose of VIP Levels

Higher VIP levels provide access to more high-speed nodes. Some nodes have level requirements.

:::
